About Giselle

Hi! I’m Giselle Sanchez Wilson, a Mexican-American, non-union performer based in Arizona. I currently attend Arizona State University, where I am pursuing a Bachelor of Music Theatre degree with a minor in Dance, and a certificate in Vocal Pedagogy. I feel incredibly lucky to pursue a passion that creates a multitude of spaces for human connection and shared experiences.

In every environment, I strive to help create a dynamic that is positive, accessible and inclusive. I am a proud recipient of the ASU Gammage HSMTA Leadership Award, and had the honor of serving as one of twelve Arizona State Thespian Officers, where I advocated for arts education and encouraged young artists across Arizona to embrace their creativity and confidence.

Off stage you can find me salsa dancing (come social dance with me!), reading, video editing, and face painting. Hope to work with you soon!
