Vocal Reel

If you cannot access the controls to this video, please follow this link to view: Giselle's Vocal Reel

Video Description:
Giselle starts the vocal reel showcasing her vocal talent alongside her acting abilities with the very emotional song, "When You're Home" from In the Heights. She utilizes her belt, mix, and head voice to give strong dynamics for such a powerful song. The next song featured is "Part of Your World" from The Little Mermaid. In this Giselle is able to showcase her belt and mix in this sweet yet powerful rendition of the song. Up next is "Falling for the Boy" from the musical Bubble Boy where Giselle is able to showcase her comedic acting alongside her vocal ability in which she's able to get laughs from the audience. The next song showcases the high parts of Giselle's vocal range where she's singing a more classic sounding song "I've Been Invited To a Party" from The Girl Who Came to Super. Giselle showcases an operatic and well-supported high note toward the end of the song. The next song is "Alyssa Greene" from The Prom where Giselle is showing off a more contemporary vocal style while also sitting down, a difficult feat. Next we have a heartwrenching rendition of "Some Thing Fall Away" from Alice By Heart where Giselle is emotional and belting dramatically similar to our first song. Her powerful voice carries the song. We finish the video on "A Lovely Night (Reprise)" from Cinderella where we see a much softer voiced Giselle singing a romantic song showcasing her bubbly happiness and sweet voice.
